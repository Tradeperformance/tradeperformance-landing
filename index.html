Pattern Alerts

Six detectors watching
your behavior in real time.

Most traders don't notice a pattern until it's already cost them. TradePerformance runs six behavioral detectors in the background — firing alerts the moment your trading goes off-script.

Size-Up After Loss
Flags when position size increases after a losing trade — a classic revenge pattern.
Revenge Trading
Detects rapid re-entry after a loss — trading emotion, not setup.
Streak Tilt
Win or loss streaks that historically precede your worst sessions.
Overtrading
Trade frequency above your personal baseline — quantity over quality signal.
Drawdown Pace
Drawdown accumulating faster than your historical average — circuit breaker signal.
Off-Playbook Trades
Trades tagged "random" accumulating — your discipline score dropping in real time.

FAQ

Common questions

Can I try it before buying?
+
Yes — the live demo on this page lets you explore the full app with sample data before spending a dollar. No sign-up required. See exactly what you're getting before you commit.
I'm already paying for a journal. Is switching worth it?
+
If you're paying $40–80/mo on a competitor, you recoup the $150 cost in 2–4 months — then it's free every year after. You get voice logging and AI screenshot reading most competitors don't have, plus BYOK data ownership. CSV import brings your history across in minutes.
Does this work with my broker and trading platform?
+
TradePerformance works with any broker or platform. Log CVD, order flow context, and session labels natively. Direct broker sync is in active development — for now, CSV export from your platform imports cleanly with automatic column mapping for most major brokers.
How does voice logging work?
+
Activate it mid-session and speak your trade — setup name, direction, what you saw. AI structures it instantly. Drop in a chart screenshot and it reads entry/exit levels, indicator readings, and key levels directly from the image. Simple trade: under 10 seconds. With screenshot: under 30 seconds.
What does BYOK mean for my data?
+
Bring Your Own Key. You connect your OpenAI or Anthropic API key — your AI conversations go directly to your provider, never through our servers. Your trade data isn't used to train anything. You pay your provider at standard rates, no TradePerformance markup.
Is this really a one-time payment? No catches?
+
Really one-time. $150, own it forever. All V1 features and updates included at no extra cost. A V2 AI upgrade may be offered as an optional add-on later — your V1 license stays fully functional regardless.
Can I run multiple accounts — prop firm, personal, evaluation?
+
Unlimited accounts — Live, Funded, Evaluation, Demo, Backtest — all in one license. Switch between them or view analytics across all simultaneously. The Risk tab tracks prop firm pass probability and daily loss limits per account.
How does the prop firm pass probability work?
+
It runs a Monte Carlo simulation using your actual trade history — win rate, average winner/loser, and drawdown profile — across thousands of simulated trading paths against your eval's specific rules (profit target, max drawdown, daily loss limit). The output is a probability that a trader with your edge passes the eval. It updates with every trade you log.
